# ARASH — Built from curiosity

## Portfolio idea

My portfolio connects my interests in human-centered products, scientific computing, and exploratory software. The required About, Skills, and Projects sections are directly accessible from a conventional scrolling layout.

## Creative feature: the Curiosity Engine

Visitors can drag a 3D particle sculpture and transform it between three dimensions. Each form links to a part of my real work: Haven's connected care routines, physics and quantum experiments, and my broader GitHub explorations. Visible hints invite interaction. Keyboard controls, reduced motion, pause, and direct dimension buttons make the experience accessible without relying on drag gestures.
